About

Hello, I’m Januki De Silva

I am a third-year Fashion Design student working at the meeting point of emotion, narrative and material. Every collection begins with a story or a feeling, and I build the garment around it through research, textile experimentation and visual development.

I am drawn to the way clothing can carry meaning beyond the surface. Through manipulated fabrics, layered surfaces and considered silhouettes, I turn abstract experiences into wearable form.

My goal is to become a designer whose work challenges conventional ideas of beauty and gives people a way to express who they are.
